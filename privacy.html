Privacy

Privacy matters.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Website

This site is designed as a simple static website. You can browse its public pages without creating an account. The website does not intentionally publish your contact email address.

Contact form

When you submit the contact form, the information you provide, including your name, email address, inquiry type, subject and message, is processed by Formspree so the message can be delivered to the configured Masum Labs inbox. Formspree may process technical information according to its own privacy practices.

Products

Privacy practices can differ by product and version. Please consult the privacy information provided with each browser extension before installation or use.

Third-party stores

Extension installation links lead to the official Firefox Add-ons and Microsoft Edge Add-ons stores. Their own privacy policies and terms apply when you use those services.

Data minimization

Masum Labs aims to collect only information needed to provide a requested interaction or service. We do not use the contact form to publicly display submitted information.

Updates

This policy may be updated as the website and products evolve. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
