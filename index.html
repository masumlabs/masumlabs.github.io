Frequently asked questions

Are your extensions free?

Pricing is always shown on the official store page of each extension. We never take payments through this website.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Our extensions process data locally on your device and do not send it to our servers. They contain no analytics or trackers. See our privacy policy.

Does it work on mobile?

Our extensions are lightweight and built for small screens too. They work in mobile browsers that support extensions, such as Firefox for Android. Support varies by browser and device, so check the compatibility note on each store listing.

Which browsers do you support?

Firefox and Microsoft Edge today. Chrome versions are on the way.
